What are you actually selling?

The Fundamental Entity question every MedTech founder skips

Alok Mishra  ·  Adjunct Associate Professor, Duke-NUS Medical School

Almost every MedTech founder, when asked “what are you selling?”, answers with a description of their device. Those are products. They’re not businesses. The gap between the two is where most MedTech startups quietly bleed out — not because the technology failed, but because they never decided who they are in the eyes of the person writing the check. This is the Fundamental Entity problem.

Is Your Innovation a Fit?

The question every medtech founder needs to answer before going to market

Chaitanya Nimmakayala  ·  Founder, future.med Connect

One of the first questions we ask any medtech founder is: what problem is your technology solving? If the answer is about how cool and innovative the technology is — you probably need to spend more time inside the hospital and OT. Founders who have spent years perfecting a technology naturally see the world through it. The innovation is ready. The clinical need is obvious to them. But the market sees it differently.
